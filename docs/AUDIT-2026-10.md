# Audit and decisions: Product Evolution brief (October 2026)

## What the app actually did (confirmed in the code and in a browser)

| Area | Found | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| First run | The first screen asked an expert question ("Who's running your show?", pick Ogilvy/Hormozi/Vaynerchuk) before anything about the business. | `views/chat.js` picker |
| Idea | No approval moment. A chat "plan" card went straight to *Create this show*, which wrote the strategy **and ~15 posts** in one go (4 weeks × 4 channels). | `campaign.js createShow`, `model.js planSlots` |
| Trust | **Real AI errors became fake success.** Strategy and posts silently fell back to demo drafts (`offlineStrategy`/`offlineAsset`), and the show was marked *ready*. Demo content had no label. | `campaign.js` catch blocks |
| Progress | Show page was a week-by-week list of 15 rows with a thin progress bar. No "do this next", no outcomes. | `views/shows.js` |
| Creative | Photo, text-on-image, caption and ChatGPT prompts all existed, but split across two tabs, with the prompts more prominent than the real post. | `views/sheets.js` |
| Navigation | Four tabs (Showrunner, Today, Shows, Me). Today and Shows overlapped; Me was a settings page in the main nav. | `main.js TABS` |

## What we changed (functional, tested)

1. **Mission-first start.** A new chat opens with *"What are we making happen?"*: four big choices (Fill bookings, Get more customers, Sell something, Promote an event), or type it. Then **at most two questions**, one at a time: the business (asked once, ever) and what to promote (with "You pick"). A typed question ending in "?" skips straight to normal chat. Measured: **3 inputs and about 5 s to an idea** in demo mode.
2. **The big reveal.** ONE concept, shown as a poster: name, example hook, the idea, the message, who it's for, why it'll work, the look, and the channels. **Let's make it / Different angle / Tweak it.** The Showrunner style is a one-tap switch on the idea (*Try The Strategist / The Closer / The Creator*), chosen automatically from the goal at first. A new AI task, `concept`, does the strategic work; the approved concept **is** the strategy, so what you approve is exactly what gets built.
3. **A workload you can keep.** After approval: *How much can you post?* Light (~2 a week, the default), Steady (3–4) or All in. The planner thins the schedule but always keeps Hook, Show and Convert.
4. **Honest generation.** Posts carry `source: ai | demo | failed`. No AI connected → *demo* drafts, labelled everywhere. AI connected but failed → *failed*: empty, never faked, with **Try again** (per post and per show). Show status is *writing*, *ready* or *partial*. Chat and idea errors show an error card with Try again instead of a canned answer.
5. **The journey.** Show page: poster → **Next mission** (exactly one: retry, *make your first post*, or the next post) → five real milestones → Hook / Show / Convert stages with missions and clear status pills (Draft, Demo draft, Ready, Posted, Got responses, Late, Retry).
6. **One creative flow.** Post sheet → **Create**: the finished post preview, then Add photo / **Save image**, the caption with Copy, text-on-image settings folded away, and ChatGPT moved to *More ways to make it*. **I posted it** replaces "Mark done".
7. **Outcomes, no fake analytics.** A day after a post goes out, Home asks: *Did it get any replies, inquiries or bookings?* (Yes, with a rough count / Not yet / Skip). Results go into the show chat's prompt so the Showrunner builds on what worked.
8. **Personality.** An original Showrunner mascot (idle, thinking, happy; blinks; respects reduced motion), a personality block in the prompts (warm, witty, recommends one thing), small celebrations **only for real milestones**: idea approved, first creative, first post out, Hook posted, first real response.
9. **Simpler navigation.** Three tabs: **Home · Create · Shows**. Me (business profile, favourite Showrunner, plan, settings) moved behind the profile button. First visit lands on Create; returning owners land on Home.

## What we deliberately did not do, and why

- **No integrated image generation.** There's no image API in the stack, and faking one would break trust. The first-party photo + text post is real and exportable; ChatGPT stays as a clearly labelled fallback.
- **No XP, streaks or daily badges.** Milestones are tied to outcomes, not app opens.
- **No archetype-only rename.** We use *The Strategist / The Closer / The Creator* as the main label, with "Ogilvy style" and the others in small print, and keep the existing "not affiliated" note.
- **No redesign of Pro.** `/pro/` is untouched apart from the plan gate from the previous iteration. `campaign.json` is unchanged; new fields are additive.
- **No Showroom or community.** Out of scope.

## Still open: the next three priorities

1. **Run `npm run eval` with the real model** and tune the `concept` prompt until 4 out of 5 ideas would be approved as they are (concept quality is now the whole product).
2. **Hosted page and form** (Connect plan): the landing page as a Webshow-hosted page whose submissions become real outcomes automatically.
3. **Pre-beta hardening:** a real rate limit (KV), auth with server storage (the local-only data can be lost), and error tracking.
